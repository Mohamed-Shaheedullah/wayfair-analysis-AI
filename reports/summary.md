# Wayfair e-commerce sales review, 2024–2025

## Three key findings

1. **More completed orders drove most of the growth.** Known-price merchandise revenue rose **20.9%**, from $841,136 in 2024 to $1,016,985 in 2025, and exceeded the prior year in all 12 matching months. Completed orders rose **18.7%**, from 3,867 to 4,589, while average order value for fully priced completed orders rose **2.0%**, from $219.01 to $223.36. [Annual metrics](metrics.md) · [Monthly revenue](monthly_revenue.md)
2. **November was the strongest revenue month in both years.** Known-price merchandise revenue reached $99,725 in November 2024 and $127,233 in November 2025. Outdoor's share of revenue peaked in June in both years, and Decor's share peaked in November 2024 and December 2025. These patterns come from two years of sales. [Monthly revenue](monthly_revenue.md) · [Category analysis](category_analysis.md)
3. **Furniture led category revenue and had the highest return rate.** It generated $318,593 in known-price merchandise revenue over two years. Of 1,007 distinct orders containing Furniture, 118 were recorded as returned (**11.72%**). Return status is recorded for the whole order, so the data cannot identify the returned item within a multi-category order. [Category analysis](category_analysis.md) · [Category outcomes](category_outcomes.md)

## Two recommendations for the following year's plan

1. **Plan inventory and fulfilment capacity ahead of November.** Use the June Outdoor and year-end Decor patterns to time promotions. Check current unit sales before committing stock for each category.
2. **Investigate Furniture returns before choosing a fix.** Review return records and collect item-level reasons to identify recurring issues by product. Use those findings to choose a change to test, then track the return rate for orders containing Furniture.

**Scope and validation.** Revenue covers known-price merchandise lines on completed orders and excludes shipping. Another 176 completed lines had missing prices. Monthly, category and order totals reconciled to **$1,858,121.00** across the two years. The data contains **9,207 distinct orders**, of which **8,456** were completed.
